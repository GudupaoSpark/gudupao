About Gudupao

We are a group of passionate young people dedicated to changing the world through technology and making innovation more fun.

Team Introduction

Gudupao Spark was established on January 31, 2023, at 4:25 PM in Huadu District, Guangzhou, Guangdong Province, China. It started as a tech studio created by a group of children who loved Minecraft to maintain their Minecraft server. The original Gudupao Studio has now grown into Gudupao Spark Inc., and we're still growing! Keep going!!!
