Development Timeline

Jan 31, 2023

Initial Establishment

On Jan 31, 2023, at 4:25 PM, Gudupao Studio (predecessor of Gudupao Spark Inc.) was established in Huadu, Guangzhou, founded by a group of middle school students who loved playing Minecraft

Mar 1, 2023

New Name, New Logo

In Mar 2023, the new name Gudupao Spark Studio was born (second phase Mar-Jul), along with a new logo

Aug 14, 2023

Reorganization After Internal Split

The remaining members formed a new studio continuing as Gudupao Spark Studio (third phase)

Nov 1, 2023

Period of Uncertainty

During this time, the studio lacked clear development goals (fourth phase Aug to Nov 2023, also known as the uncertainty phase)

Dec 1, 2023

Beginning of Independent Development

Proposed the Xianyu Analysis Project, began the period of independent algorithm software development, established feasible phase goals, and continued development. The studio officially entered its fifth phase (Dec 2023 to present)

Jan 30, 2024

Xianyu Analysis Release

Xianyu Analysis was released after two months of effort by three members of Gudupao Studio. The promotional video premiered on Bilibili (B-site), marking the success of Gudupao Spark Studio's independent development path!!!

Jan 31, 2024

Studio's First Anniversary

After a year of challenges, the studio celebrated its first anniversary. Throughout this year, the studio experienced difficult times and periods of uncertainty. We picked up and lit our torch in the vast snow, and we will illuminate our own future ahead.

Pick up your own torch and light up your own future!
